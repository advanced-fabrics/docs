# Community

AdvFab develops its standards and implementation in public.

## Participate

- Read the [contribution guide](https://github.com/advanced-fabrics/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [code of conduct](https://github.com/advanced-fabrics/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Propose a standards change through the [AFEP process](https://github.com/advanced-fabrics/spec/blob/main/afeps/0000-process.md).
- Follow the [roadmap](https://github.com/advanced-fabrics/community/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) and [release process](https://github.com/advanced-fabrics/community/blob/main/RELEASES.md).

## Governance

RE8CH initiated the project, which is governed independently. The current bootstrap model transitions to a Maintainer Council after the published thresholds are met. Read the [governance document](https://github.com/advanced-fabrics/community/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md) for decision rules and conflict handling.

## CNCF status

!!! info
    AdvFab is not a CNCF project and does not claim CNCF endorsement. The roadmap describes possible future Landscape and Sandbox consideration, dependent on adoption and other evidence.

Community

AdvFab develops its standards and implementation in public.

Participate

  • Read the contribution guide and code of conduct.
  • Propose a standards change through the AFEP process.
  • Follow the roadmap and release process.

Governance

RE8CH initiated the project, which is governed independently. The current bootstrap model transitions to a Maintainer Council after the published thresholds are met. Read the governance document for decision rules and conflict handling.

CNCF status

AdvFab is not a CNCF project and does not claim CNCF endorsement. The roadmap describes possible future Landscape and Sandbox consideration, dependent on adoption and other evidence.
